Yahoo

Known for its freemail services AOL and Yahoo, they provide a detailed Postmaster page with contact options for investigating false-positive spam verdicts. They offer a Senders Hub Insights Dashboard, as well as a Complaint Feedback Loop, more details can be found on the link below. As of August 2025, they also handle the incomming connections for the AT&T domains.

You have exceeded the number of messages you can send in a single connection. If you do not control the MTA, you may need to ask your email provider for adjustments.

421 4.7.0 [TSS04] Messages from 0.0.0.0 temporarily deferred due to unexpected volume or user complaints - 4.16.55.1

This could indicate one of few problems, either that your emails are generating an excessive amount of complaints from Yahoo users or Yahoo is seeing unsual traffic patterns from your mail servers. It could also mean that your email content contains objectionable content or show characteristics indicative of spam. Another possiblity is that your IP has a poor reputation

554↑
554 5.7.9 This mail has been blocked because it failed authentication checks against the sending domains DMARC policy.

The sent message failed the DMARC policy for the sending domain. If the sending domain has a reject policy set for DMARC, the message must pass DKIM or have SPF aligned to the domain with a custom Return-Path. To resolve this, you'll need to contact your email service provider to configure how to send a message that passes DMARC.

  • https://senders.yahooinc.com/smtp-error-codes#dmarc-fail
